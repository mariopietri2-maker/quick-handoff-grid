## Full description (EN)

Fresh2GO is the local food delivery app for Ioannina, Greece.

Order from nearby restaurants — pizza, souvlaki, burgers, crepes, coffee and more. Browse open stores, deals and free delivery where available.

Features:
• Discover stores near you
• Fast cart and checkout
• Cash or card payment
• Real-time order tracking
• Saved delivery address
• In-app support

Built for Ioannina — fast delivery, clear UI, local restaurants.
